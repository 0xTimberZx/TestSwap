WHAT IT IS

A testnet campaign with two parts: playing Season 1 and a content bounty paid in USDT from a fixed pool of $200 in total. The two are separate. Prizes are paid in USDT and do not depend on Season 1 points.

PART 1 · PLAY (SEASON 1)
  • Season 1 records on-chain activity: tickets, rounds played, swaps, liquidity, staking, farm and staking claims, and faucet claims. Start at timbswap.xyz/faucet, then timbswap.xyz/compete.
  • How points are weighted, and what they are worth, is not final. Points are off-chain and non-transferable, have no cash value, are recomputed whenever scoring changes, and can move down as well as up. No amount, rate or token is promised. See timbswap.xyz/quests.
PART 2 · THE CONTENT BOUNTY

What counts: original content that explains how the TimbSwap prize game works. An X thread, a short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts or YouTube), a written guide, a meme, an infographic, or a translation of the docs or a guide.

NEED AN IDEA? EXPLAIN IT WITH A SAFE
  • Your ticket is a six-character PIN.
  • The market sets the safe's code, one character per segment. Swaps turn the dial: they steer whether a segment lands on a letter or a digit. A random draw picks the exact character.
  • Match all six and the safe opens: you split the pot.
  • Your deposit sits in your own locker, not in the safe. It stays yours while the ticket plays; take it back within the refund window after the ticket's last round ends, or it lapses.
  • The protocol fills the safe from yield and fees, not from other players' deposits.

Any angle is welcome; using this idea is optional and is judged like any other entry. Keep it accurate: the code is steered and matched, never cracked or predicted.
