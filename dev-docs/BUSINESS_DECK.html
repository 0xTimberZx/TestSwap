TimbSwap · Seed Brief01 / 12

The Problem

Most DeFi rents activity.
It doesn't earn it.

Speculation-only design

The pattern that keeps failing

  • Rewards are printed, not funded — emissions dilute holders to buy short-term activity.
  • When price stalls or emissions taper, mercenary capital leaves and usage collapses.
  • Incentives are static, disconnected from whether the protocol is actually healthy.

Why existing pieces don't fix it

Each solves one slice, in isolation

  • AMMs reward liquidity — but not engagement.
  • Staking rewards holding — but is often pure inflation.
  • Prize dapps drive attention — but are detached from real yield.
  • Nobody ties them into one self-funding loop.
The Problem02 / 12

Our Thesis

Treat the incentive system itself as the product — a closed loop that recirculates real earnings.

The closest traditional analogue is a prize-linked savings account — Premium Bonds, Save-to-Win — where prizes come from interest, not deposits. TimbSwap generalizes that: every part of the protocol earns, and those earnings are routed back to the people who create them. Not another exchange — a better economic engine.

Our Thesis03 / 12

Precedent & the Open Frontier

Proven in the real world.
Unclaimed on-chain.

Proven at scale — our model

Boring, durable, enormous

  • Premium Bonds (UK). Prize-linked savings since 1956 — well over £100B held by tens of millions. Prizes from interest; principal intact. Our exact model, at national scale.
  • Loyalty as infrastructure. Airline miles and card points — the reward layer grew worth more than the airline running it. Proof an incentive system can become the primary asset.
  • Tokenized real-world yield (RWAs). Real yield is arriving on-chain — but today's RWAs stop at wrapping it. None pairs it with an engagement engine.

The extractive analogues

Huge — but they take from the player

  • Lotteries & national games. Hundreds of billions in annual play — but the house keeps the take. We pay from yield and recirculate.
  • Casinos — "the house." Always solvent, by keeping the player's money. We stay solvent without touching principal.
  • Speculative token farms. Loud, then gone — printed rewards with no earnings underneath.

The open seat

These models are proven, durable, and vast in the real world — yet nothing in crypto has ascended to a comparable share of its own space. On-chain prize-linked savings peaked in the low hundreds of millions and faded; no incentive layer has become the asset the way loyalty did in aviation. Relative to the size of crypto, the seat is still empty — and that is the seat we're building for.

Economic Design · Barriers

Guardrails for the house.
Skin in for the player.

On the game's side

Why the house can't over-extend

  • The pot is funded by yield, never new deposits.
  • A solvency stop freezes payouts at 99% of what's owed — it never writes a check it can't cash.
  • Emissions retarget to real revenue — no printing your way out.
  • Fixed total supply: 100,000,000 tokens, with no mint beyond it.
  • Foundation before flourish: core rewards funded before speculative ones.

On the player's side

Why a win is earned, not handed out

  • Skin in to sit at the table — hold the token or provide liquidity to play.
  • One dial per wallet each round — no one buys the whole board.
  • Only active trades work the dials. Passive holders don't get drafted.
  • Winners claim inside a window — collect, or it rolls back into the pot.
Economic Design · Barriers07 / 12

Risk & the Open Item

What we've closed — and what's still open.

Closed by design

Custody: non-custodial. Users hold their own keys; the protocol never takes discretionary control of funds.

Contracts: testnet-hardened, with permissionless refund and recovery paths and every action logged to live telemetry for audit.

Solvency: the barriers on the prior page make over-emission and dilution structurally impossible.

The open item GATING

Legal & regulatory. A yield-funded prize sits on the prize-linked-savings precedent — but the classification is jurisdiction-specific.

Formal counsel and likely geofencing are treated as conditions to clear before mainnet, not afterthoughts. We'd rather name it plainly than have you find it.

Long-Term Vision · Roadmap

From fixed rules to adaptive incentives.

Where the engine is headed — stated as roadmap, not as a capability we claim today.

Now · Live

Rule-based allocation

The reward cycle is deterministic and solvency-bounded — a fixed priority waterfall. Predictable and auditable by design.

Next

Instrumented signals

The protocol already emits rich telemetry — liquidity depth, swaps, staking, treasury health. Next is turning that into live signals and dashboards.

Vision

Adaptive policy

An allocation policy that tunes incentives to observed protocol health — still on-chain, still transparent, still solvency-bounded.

The same telemetry that proves solvency today is the substrate for adaptive incentives tomorrow. Every phase keeps the identical guardrails — fixed supply, the solvency stop, non-custodial. The ambition grows; the discipline doesn't move.
