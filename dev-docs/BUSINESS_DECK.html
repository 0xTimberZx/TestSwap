TimbSwap · Seed Brief01 / 09

The Concept

An exchange that pays a prize
for showing up.

The Exchange

Traders swap tokens

A conventional automated exchange. Every trade pays a small fee, exactly as a market maker's spread does. This is the revenue engine.

The Prize Layer

Activity earns a shot

Fees and the yield on capital held in the protocol fund a recurring prize pot. Active traders compete for it, round after round.

Think Premium Bonds, on rails: the prize is funded by the interest, so no one is ever paid out of another participant's deposit. A prize-linked-savings structure — a recognized, non-speculative model.

Barriers, Both Sides

Guardrails for the house.
Skin in for the player.

On the game's side

Why the house can't over-extend

  • The safe only holds what's been earned — the pot is funded by yield, never new deposits.
  • A solvency stop freezes payouts at 99% of what's owed. It never writes a check it can't cash.
  • Emissions retarget to real revenue — no printing your way out.
  • Fixed total supply: 100,000,000 tokens, with no mint beyond it.
  • Foundation before flourish: core rewards are funded before speculative ones.

On the player's side

Why a win is earned, not handed out

  • Skin in to sit at the table — you hold the token or provide liquidity to play.
  • One dial per wallet each round — no one buys the whole board.
  • You have to show up: only active trades work the dials. Passive holders don't get drafted.
  • Winners claim inside a window — collect the trophy, or it rolls back into the pot.

Getting picked isn't pure luck — it's earning a spot on the roster by playing, then being drafted into the winners when the round settles.

Where We Are06 / 09

Risk & the Open Item

What we've closed — and what's still open.

Closed by design

Custody: non-custodial. Users hold their own keys; the protocol never takes discretionary control of funds.

Contracts: testnet-hardened, with permissionless refund and recovery paths and every action logged to live telemetry for audit.

Solvency: the barriers on the prior page make over-emission and dilution structurally impossible.

The open item GATING

Legal & regulatory. A prize mechanic funded by yield sits on the prize-linked-savings precedent — but the classification is jurisdiction-specific.

Formal counsel and likely geofencing are treated as conditions to clear before mainnet, not afterthoughts. We'd rather name it plainly than have you find it.
