Settle

Runs the whole close-out back-to-back so the six locks land well inside the block-hash window. You can still fire the steps individually below.

Fallback needs no secret and works once the reveal window (64 blocks) has passed, but only while the lock block is still inside the 256-block hash horizon. On Arbitrum block.number is the L1 block number, so those are L1 blocks: roughly 13 minutes until the fallback opens and 51 minutes until the table can no longer be locked. Use it if the reveal is lost or late.
